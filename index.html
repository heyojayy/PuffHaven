Puff Haven keeps Cobblemon's core loop intact and cushions the rest — no lag spikes, no grief, just a world that wants you to stay a while.

🎣

Every biome spawns true to its type — hunt shinies, legendaries, and regional forms across a hand-tuned overworld.

⚔️

Battle

Trade

How to Start

From download to your first battle.

1

Install Cobblemon

Grab the Cobblemon mod for your Minecraft launcher — Fabric or Forge, whichever you run.

2

Add the server

Copy the IP below into your multiplayer list and hit connect. No whitelist, no waiting.

3

Pick your starter

Visit Professor's Hollow to choose your first partner and receive your one Poké Ball.

4

Say hi in Discord

Join the community for trade meetups, event pings, and screenshots of everyone's builds.
